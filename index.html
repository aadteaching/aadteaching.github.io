How does the free consultation work?

A relaxed 30-minute video call with an academic director. We talk goals, marks and upcoming tests, then recommend a plan. No obligation.

How do online sessions work?

Live, one-on-one video with a shared digital whiteboard. Students join from anywhere, and we schedule in your time zone.

How much does it cost?

Every student's needs are different, so we tailor a plan and walk you through clear options in your free consultation. No registration fees and no long contracts.

Do you help with research and grad school applications?

Yes. Published researchers mentor students through research projects, paper writing and submission, and Master's and PhD applications.
